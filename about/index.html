Why the workbooks exist

My own training exposed the gap between recognizing a multiple-choice answer and explaining a safe decision to an instructor or examiner. The workbook series is designed for repetition, written reasoning, scenario practice, and the follow-up question: “Why?”

Professional background

I have led a small automotive-services business as general manager since 2017, with responsibility for a team, forecasting, and the operating P&L. Before that I worked in dealership sales, finance, and account development. That background informs the operator discipline behind the products. It is not aircraft-sales, turbine, or flight-instruction experience, and I do not present it as such.
