How FeedFare Works

FeedFare pairs walking with a personal screen-time budget: walk, record earned minutes, and choose to stay within your goal. The minutes are an in-app motivation tool, not permission enforced by iOS.

1. Set a daily movement goal

Pick the step target that matches your real life. The point is not to cosplay as an ultramarathoner. The point is to create a threshold that nudges you out of passive scrolling and into motion you can actually sustain.

2. Earn minutes by walking

Use step tracking and earned minutes as a personal reminder to walk before you scroll. Do not rely on FeedFare to control access to other apps.

3. Build a healthier loop

The app is designed to make better phone habits feel concrete. You see the trade in front of you: more steps, more minutes, less mindless consumption. Over time, that makes your screen use feel intentional instead of automatic.

FeedFare FAQ

Is FeedFare a screen time blocker?

Use FeedFare as a step-tracking and self-accountability tool, not a guaranteed app blocker. Do not rely on it for operating-system restrictions. You remain responsible for following your screen-time budget.

How does FeedFare count steps?

Check the permissions requested by your installed version in the app and iOS Settings. Apple Health or Apple Watch syncing should not be assumed.
