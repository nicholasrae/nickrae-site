Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 4, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how FeedFare handles information when you use our iOS app.

Information We Collect

  • Step count data — used to calculate earned minutes toward your personal walking goals.
  • App preferences and progress — your selected app slots, step goals, and streak history are stored locally on your device.
  • Subscription information — payment processed through Apple's App Store via RevenueCat. We do not store payment details.

Step Tracking Permissions

Check the permissions requested by your installed version in the app and iOS Settings. Apple Health and Apple Watch syncing should not be assumed.

Data Storage

Streaks, goals, selected app slots, and timer history are stored locally using AsyncStorage. If you enable iCloud Backup, these local records may be included according to your iOS settings. Subscription records are also handled by Apple and RevenueCat; they are not covered by a claim that all data stays on your device.

Third-Party Services

  • RevenueCat — subscription management and purchase-history handling. Privacy Policy.
  • Apple App Store — payment processing. Privacy Policy.
  • Expo Notifications — schedules local reminders on your device, subject to your notification permissions.

Data Deletion

Deleting the app removes its local records from that installation. This is not a request to delete purchase or subscription records held by Apple or RevenueCat, and it does not cancel a subscription. For questions about other records, contact us below.

Children's Privacy

FeedFare is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.
