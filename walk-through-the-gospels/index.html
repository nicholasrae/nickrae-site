A simple rhythm: read, find, reflect.

Room to work

Each puzzle uses a 10 × 10 grid and nine words. Grid letters are 18 point, with supporting text at least 16 point.

A little extra, if you want it

Optional coordinate-based hidden reflections add a second step after the word search. A worked example shows how to use them; you can also just enjoy finding the words.

Your Bible alongside

Scripture references invite you to read the passage in your own Bible. The brief introductions and reflections are original writing, not Scripture quotations.

Answers at full size

All 60 puzzles have full-size answer keys. Check a word, finish a puzzle, or move on at your own pace.

For readers who enjoy Bible-themed puzzles

This is a word-search collection, not a Bible replacement or a verse-by-verse study guide. Its journey is selective: it does not combine every event into a complete Gospel harmony.

Use it for a quiet puzzle break, alongside personal Bible reading, or as a paperback gift for someone who enjoys word searches.

Before you choose a copy

Do I need to solve the hidden reflections?

No. They are optional. The nine-word search on each puzzle stands on its own, and a worked example explains the coordinate step if you want to try it.

Which Bible translation does it use?

The book gives Scripture references for you to look up in your own Bible. Its introductions and reflections are not quotations from a Bible translation.

What edition is available?

The paperback is available on Amazon. It has 128 pages in an 8.5 × 11-inch format. ISBN: 9798177956763. Published September 29, 2026.
