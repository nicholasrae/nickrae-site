Privacy policy

NickRae.net works without optional measurement. Google's analytics and advertising-measurement tag loads only after you choose “Allow measurement.”

What this site stores

The site stores your optional analytics choice in your browser's local storage so the privacy banner does not ask on every page. The embedded Kit signup form may also use browser storage to recognize form visitors and previous subscribers. Static tools, including the flight-hour calculator, run in your browser and do not send calculator inputs to NickRae.net.

Optional Google analytics and advertising measurement

If you allow measurement, the site loads Google's tag for aggregate traffic and outbound-conversion measurement. Events may include the page path, clicked link, CTA label, placement, destination host, and product category. A calculator-use event records that the calculator was used, without the numbers you entered or the calculated results. Ad personalization signals are disabled by this site's loader. Google may still process technical information under its own policies.

If you choose “Necessary only,” the Google tag does not load on a fresh page. If you withdraw consent after allowing it, optional event measurement is disabled immediately, the loaded tag element is removed, and known Google measurement cookies are cleared. Reloading keeps the site in necessary-only mode. You can change the choice below.

External services and links

The site links to services such as Amazon, Gumroad, Apple, YouTube, X, LinkedIn, GitHub, and standalone product sites. Those services receive normal request information when you follow a link and apply their own privacy policies. Purchases and downloads are processed by the destination service, not by NickRae.net.

Email signup and free private pilot cards

The site includes a Kit signup form for five free Private Pilot Oral Scenario Cards and occasional aviation study tips from Nick Rae. When you submit the form, Kit processes your email address and sends a confirmation email. Confirming your subscription gives you access to the free PDF. You can unsubscribe using the link in any newsletter email.

The embedded form loads Kit's scripts and may send technical and form-visit information, such as your IP address, browser information, page address and referrer, to Kit when the form loads. This form operates separately from the optional Google measurement choice. Kit handles this information under its privacy policy. For questions or removal from Nick's email list, contact nick@nickrae.net.

Emails and direct contact

If you email Nick directly, the message and address are used to respond and maintain the conversation. Calculator inputs are not uploaded.

Product-specific policies

Apps and products may have separate privacy and terms pages describing their own data handling. Those product policies control for the product itself.

Questions or deletion requests

Email nick@nickrae.net. Because this is primarily a static site, NickRae.net generally does not maintain a user account database.
