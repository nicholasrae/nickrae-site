Walk Through the Gospels is now available in paperback

A new Bible word-search collection, separate from my aviation workbook shelf.

Walk Through the Gospels: Large-Print Bible Word Searches is now available on Amazon in paperback. It follows selected Gospel scenes from the Nativity to the empty tomb through 60 distinct puzzles.

What’s inside

Each puzzle has a 10 × 10 grid and nine words to find. The grid letters are 18 point, with supporting text at least 16 point. Optional coordinate-based hidden reflections offer an extra step, and a worked example explains how they work.

The book includes 60 full-size answer keys. Scripture references invite you to open your own Bible; the introductions and reflections are original writing, not Scripture quotations. This is a selective journey, not a complete Gospel harmony.

A different corner of the bookshelf

The aviation workbooks remain the main focus of this site. This release is a separate kind of book: a paperback for readers who enjoy Bible-themed word searches, whether for a quiet break or alongside their own Bible reading.

See the book

The paperback is 128 pages, in an 8.5 × 11-inch format. The product page has the cover, details, and a link to the Amazon listing.
