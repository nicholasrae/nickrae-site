The right fit

What you’ll work on.

For airplane single-engine land (ASEL) commercial applicants working through knowledge review and checkride preparation. It can also help organize the questions you want to discuss before your next training lesson.

One useful study session

Give the question some work.

  1. Start with a training objective

    Choose one knowledge area or maneuver from your instructor’s plan. Make a short list of questions you cannot yet explain clearly.

  2. Pair the written and spoken answer

    Work the practice question, then say the reasoning out loud. Note where you need a regulation, aircraft limitation or current ACS reference.

  3. Debrief after the lesson

    Compare your written preparation with the instructor’s feedback. Turn the remaining gaps into the next small study session.
