From reflection to a plan

The guided reset is a manual exercise. You write the answers and review what to save; no AI is required.

Morning: notice the pattern

Use prompts to reflect on habits, identity and direction. Pause and return when you are ready.

Daytime: check in

Record short observations as the day unfolds. Optional reminders help make room for the practice.

Evening: choose tomorrow

Connect your reflections to a yearly goal, a monthly project and a few actions for tomorrow. Review the plan before saving it.
