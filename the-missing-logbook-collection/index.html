The Last Good Look

Case 1, puzzle 1 · Paperback page 7

A guest’s snapshot shows Ada’s wrapped scrapbook beside covered display bases. Where does the ten o’clock snapshot place the logbook?

  1. Find all 12 words. They run horizontally, vertically or diagonally in either direction and can share letters.
  2. Read the unused letters in outlined row 10 from left to right. Split them into a word of three letters and a word of five.
