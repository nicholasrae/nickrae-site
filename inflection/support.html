Pre-release support

Inflection Point’s App Store release is in preparation. Status and planned offer checked October 13, 2026; the product site carries the latest information.

Can I download it now?

Not yet. No public release date has been announced. The launch site explains how to request an email when it becomes available.

What is the planned price?

One lifetime purchase, planned at US $14.99 in the United States. There is no free trial, ongoing free tier or recurring subscription. Apple’s localized price will be shown before purchase in other storefronts.

Which devices are planned?

iPhone with iOS 26 or later. Manual planning, the guided reset and Quick Log do not require Apple Intelligence. Optional AI features have additional device, language, region and availability requirements described on the product site.

Where are the current policies?

Read the maintained privacy policy and terms for the planned native release and differences from earlier versions. The older August 2026 notices on nickrae.net are retained as labeled archives.

What about an earlier version or purchase?

Email the developer with your app version, device and question. Do not send private journal entries, passwords or payment details. The planned native release keeps export and recovery of existing data available without a purchase.
