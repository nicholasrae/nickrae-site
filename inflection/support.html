Pre-release support

Inflection Point is coming soon and awaiting App Store release. The guidance below describes planned launch behavior.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I get started?

At launch, the onboarding flow is planned to begin with the Anti-Vision exercise, followed by the daily quest system.

Is there a subscription?

No. At launch, Inflection Point is planned to use a 3-day access preview, then a free plan or optional $14.99 lifetime unlock. No subscription or auto-renewal is planned.

How do I restore my purchase?

After App Store release, open Inflection Point, go to Settings, and tap "Restore Purchases." Make sure you are signed in with the same Apple ID used for the original lifetime unlock purchase.

Is my data backed up?

The planned release stores journal entries and progress locally on the device. They may be included in iCloud Backup when that device-level setting is enabled.

Can I troubleshoot the app now?

Not yet. The app is awaiting App Store release. After launch, verified troubleshooting steps will be published here; pre-release questions can be sent below.
