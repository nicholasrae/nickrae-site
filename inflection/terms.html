Draft pre-release terms

Last updated: August 1, 2026 · Awaiting App Store release

Inflection Point is not currently available for download. These draft terms describe the planned launch conditions and will be reviewed against the released build before publication.

Use of the App

At launch, Inflection Point is planned as a personal-development app for iOS intended for personal, non-commercial use.

Planned Access Preview and Lifetime Unlock

  • At launch, Inflection Point is planned to include a 3-day access preview.
  • After the planned preview, users will be able to continue on a free plan or purchase an optional $14.99 lifetime unlock.

At launch, users will retain ownership of journal entries and personal content created in the app. Nick Rae will not claim ownership over that content.

Disclaimer

The planned app is for personal-development purposes and will not be a substitute for professional advice. Final launch terms and warranty language will be published with the released build.

Changes to Terms
